The journey

Thirty years of Windows. Then one clean install.

I am not a Linux professional, and I am not a developer. I am a gamer who got curious, moved his whole setup to Linux, and started filming what happened.

"I am learning as I go, picking up tips from others, and improving my setup along the way."

Read the full story
  1. Before

    Three decades on Windows

    Gaming, tinkering and the odd fresh install, always with Windows underneath.

  2. 2025

    The switch

    Left Windows behind completely. Not a dual boot, not a spare machine. Everything moved.

  3. Now

    Fedora and KineticWE

    Fedora as the base, KineticWE and Noctalia on Wayland, with a Mac around for the rare thing that still needs one.

  4. Sharing

    Filming the process

    Videos about the wins, the mistakes, the games and the fixes. The tools are a side effect of solving my own problems.
